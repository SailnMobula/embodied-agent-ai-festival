## Same description, you and the robot

<JointsAndLinks />

- **Joint** — a place that turns.
- **Link** — the rigid piece between two joints. Your bones.
- **Angle** — how far a joint is turned.

## Angles in, position out

<ForwardKinematicsDemo client:visible />

- Pure geometry. No AI. This is **forward kinematics**.
- The last point is the **end effector** — the hand.
- Every joint drags everything after it.
- A degree at the shoulder moves the hand further than a degree at the wrist.

## Teaching is recording

Press **Record**, move the sliders, press **Replay**.

- A taught motion is angles plus timestamps.
- Replay is exact. Every time.
- It looks at nothing. It reacts to nothing.

> **Live demo — the robot waves.** Same trick, real hardware: a file of joint angles, played back.
> Run it twice, it is identical to the degree.

## Training is not a list

Balance is a reaction. No file answers a shove.

<LocomotionTraining />

- Reward: upright, moving forward. Penalty: falling, flailing, burning current.
- Nobody writes a gait.
- Vary mass, friction, delay, terrain — then move it to real hardware. That gap is **sim-to-real**.

Out comes a **policy**: sensors in, joint targets out, fifty times a second. Ask why it moved that
ankle — there is no answer to read.

## On this robot

- **Wave** — taught. A recording.
- **Stand, balance, walk** — trained. A policy.
